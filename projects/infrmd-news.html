infrmd.news

Published: July 27, 2026

infrmd.news is a Nigeria-first news aggregation platform for Nigerians at home and abroad who want to understand important stories without searching through several news websites or reading the same update repeatedly. It brings major Nigerian news, government and policy updates, business, technology, security, infrastructure, sports, entertainment, and diaspora-relevant stories into one clean, easy-to-scan feed.

When several trusted publishers or official sources cover the same event, infrmd.news can bring that coverage together as one story. Readers receive a concise, neutral summary and the key details, with clear links to the original sources for deeper reading and verification.

What It Does

The public website gives readers a regularly updated feed, dedicated story pages, category browsing, original source links, and the ability to save stories for later. Its publishing system keeps source collection, writing, fact-checking, and approval organised so only completed stories reach the public feed.

Key Features

  • A Nigeria-first feed for major stories and public-interest updates
  • Short, neutral summaries designed for quick understanding
  • Story grouping that reduces repeated coverage of the same event
  • Visible links to trusted publishers and official sources
  • Category pages for focused browsing across Nigerian news topics
  • Individual story pages with key details and source information
  • Saved stories that readers can return to later
  • Optional AI drafting assistance with human approval before publication

Design Approach

The interface is designed for quick scanning and comfortable reading on desktop and mobile. A restrained layout, clear hierarchy, and deep green accents give the product a modern Nigerian identity while keeping attention on each story, its key facts, and its sources.

Why I Built It

Nigerian news is spread across many publishers, and important updates are often repeated across several websites. I built infrmd.news to make staying informed faster and less overwhelming. It is not intended to replace original journalism; it acts as a discovery and comprehension layer that helps readers understand the main story quickly and continue to the original reporting whenever they want more detail.
